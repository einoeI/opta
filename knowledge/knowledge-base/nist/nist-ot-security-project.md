# NIST CSRC Operational Technology Security project page

## Summary

NIST's project hub for OT security. Its one recent update announces the comment period for the SP 800-82r4 initial public draft.
The "30 November" date the user noted is the close of that comment period in 2026. It is not a release date for a new final version.
Rev. 4 is still a draft as of 2026-11-09, and Rev. 3 remains the current final edition.

## Key facts

- The page shows "Created June 04, 2021, Updated September 21, 2026".
- Its Recent Updates entry, dated September 21, 2026, announces a comment period for the SP 800-82r4 initial public draft "open through November 30th".
- The entry gives no year after "November 30th". The year 2026 comes from the Rev. 4 IPD page and from the Rev. 3 page's planning note, which says "Public comments are due by November 30, 2026" (https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r4/ipd and https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/82/r3/final, accessed 2026-11-09).
- The project page states no publication date for a final Rev. 4. Neither do the Rev. 4 IPD page or PDF.

## Verbatim

> "September 21, 2026: A comment period for the initial public draft of SP 800-82r4 (Revision 4), Guide to Operational Technology (OT) Security, is open through November 30th."

## Relevance

- Resolves the user's question about a "new version on 30 November": that date closes public comment on a draft.
- Inference: a final Rev. 4 cannot be in force on 2026-11-30, since NIST must first process comments. No source gives its timing.
- Recheck this page before any report that cites SP 800-82 as current.
