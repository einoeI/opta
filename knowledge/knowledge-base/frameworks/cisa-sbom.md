## Summary
CISA's SBOM landing page, defining SBOM and VEX and linking resources. It announces 2026 updates to the Minimum Elements for an SBOM, a joint guidance adding data fields, practices and processes.

## Key facts
- An SBOM is described as a nested inventory of the components that make up software.
- A VEX document is described as an attestation, a form of security advisory, indicating whether products are affected by known vulnerabilities.
- Resources listed include "2026 Updates to Minimum Elements", "2025 Minimum Elements" draft guidance, SBOM FAQ, SBOM Sharing Primer, SBOM Sharing Roles and Considerations.

## Verbatim
- "An SBOM is a nested inventory, a list of ingredients that make up software components."
- "A VEX document is an attestation, a form of a security advisory that indicates whether a product or products are affected by a known vulnerability or vulnerabilities."

## Relevance
Reference definitions for reports and for vendor-SBOM requests (OWASP OT #3). The 2026 Minimum Elements document itself was not read.
