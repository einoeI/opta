## Summary
OWASP full-stack bill-of-materials standard, published as ECMA-424, with work driven by OWASP and Ecma TC54. It covers SBOM, SaaSBOM, CBOM, VEX, HBOM and AI/ML-BOM. The site states it surfaces risk for IT and OT assets.

## Key facts
- Latest specification tag on 2026-11-09 is 1.7.2; previous 1.7.1 (2026-06-02) and 1.6.2.
- Standard identity: ECMA-424.
- Capabilities listed include SBOM, SaaSBOM, CBOM, VEX, HBOM, AI/ML-BOM.
- Site claims compatibility with over 260 tools across 20+ languages (project claim).

## Verbatim
- "CycloneDX is enterprise ready and surfaces risk for IT and OT assets."

## Relevance
Recommended SBOM interchange format: EMBA, Syft, Dependency-Track and Phoenix Contact's own workflow all use it (OWASP OT #3).
